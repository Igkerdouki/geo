How this becomes real

Inputs: selected location, application scenario, weather conditions, model assumptions.

Computation: documented model of dispersion or deposition, with calibration and limitations made explicit.

Output: comparison between scenarios, map overlays, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty notes.

02 · COMPUTE

Dispersion modeling

Defined inputs, documented assumptions and scenario comparisons; not an unvalidated claim of personal exposure.
