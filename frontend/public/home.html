How askgaia investigates

Four agents. One question. Every answer sourced.

  1. LocateFinds the school or address and the square-mile sections around it.
  2. RecordsPulls 11 years of state pesticide records and creek sampling near it.
  3. WindFetches the wind and runs a Gaussian drift model toward the place.
  4. VerifyAttaches sources and separates what was observed, modelled and uncertain.
